Morphology-Aware ECG Denoising Using Deep Neural Networks for Arrhythmia Detection

Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET) • 2025–Present
Supervisor: Dr. Md. Kamrul Hasan, Professor, Department of EEE, BUET

Ambulatory ECG recordings are frequently corrupted by baseline wander, muscle artifacts, and electrode motion that distort critical P-Q-R-S-T wave morphology. This research develops MorphoUFormer, a multiscale deep architecture with gated skip connections, a Transformer bottleneck, and a multi-component morphology-preserving loss to clean signals while preserving vital diagnostic waveforms for downstream arrhythmia classification.

ONGOING RESEARCH

Weakly Supervised Temporal Action Localization for Non-Intrusive Load Monitoring

Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET) • 2025–Present

Non-Intrusive Load Monitoring (NILM) aims to disaggregate individual appliance electricity consumption from aggregate household smart-meter readings. This research investigates Weakly Supervised Temporal Action Localization (WTAL) to accurately identify activation boundaries and duration from aggregate power series using only coarse household-level presence indicators, eliminating costly sub-metered annotations.
